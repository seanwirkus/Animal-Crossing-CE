Core Features

🎒

Inventory System

Drag-and-drop interface with purchasable pocket expansions across four upgrade tiers. Persistent inventory management with seamless item organization.

🔨

Crafting Stations

Multiple stations including Workbench, Forge, Cooking Stove, Sewing Table, and Potion Bench with themed recipe sets and progression unlocks.

🏝️

Island Generation

Procedural island generation with persistent player islands. Build your dream island with custom terrain and decorations.

📱

NookPhone

Integrated navigation tools including minimap, tool ring, and NookPhone with shopping interface and quest tracking.

😊

Social Features

Emote wheel with animations and interactive player expressions. Engage with friends in a vibrant social experience.

🌱

Recipe Discovery

Server-driven recipe unlocking system based on milestones and player progression. Discover new crafting possibilities as you play.

Design Philosophy

Animal Crossing CE maintains a unified cream and beige aesthetic matching the original game's visual style. Every system is built with attention to detail, from the drag-and-drop inventory mechanics to the server-driven progression systems. The goal is to capture the cozy, relaxing essence of Animal Crossing while leveraging Roblox's multiplayer capabilities.
